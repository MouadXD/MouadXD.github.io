## Overview

Describe the authorized lab, its scope, and the objective.

## Methodology

Document the enumeration and testing process, explaining why each step was taken.

## Finding

Describe the vulnerability, the evidence, and its security impact.

## Proof of Concept

Include sanitized requests, responses, commands, or screenshots from the lab.

## Remediation

Explain how the vulnerability can be fixed and how the fix can be verified.

## References

Link to relevant official documentation and security guidance.
